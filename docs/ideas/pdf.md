# PDF viewer: left for later

Explored, not decided or built. The viewer: [features/pdf-viewer.md](../features/pdf-viewer.md),
[ADR 0192](../adr/0192-pdf-viewer.md).

## Reading
- **Remembered position and zoom** per file (as the ebook reader remembers its position).
- **Read Aloud** of the page text.
- **Dark or inverted pages** for the dark theme; the pages are white as printed today.
- **Two-page spreads** and **rotating** pages by the user.
- **Tiles** for pages beyond the 16-megapixel cap, which are enlarged today.

## Beyond reading
- **Printing**.
- **Forms** and **annotations** other than links (showing, filling, editing).
- **PDF export from Markdown** (noted in [ADR 0058](../adr/0058-markdown-html-export.md)).
