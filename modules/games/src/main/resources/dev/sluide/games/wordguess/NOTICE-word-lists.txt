Word lists of the Word Guess game (en-allowed.txt, en-answers.txt, de-allowed.txt, de-answers.txt).
All four contain five-letter words, upper case, one per line, sorted, UTF-8, LF. In German, ss stands for the sharp s
(ß is replaced by SS before upper-casing). All sources were fetched on 2026-11-06.

=====================================================================================================================
en-allowed.txt: ENABLE2K, plus every word of en-answers.txt
=====================================================================================================================
Source:  https://raw.githubusercontent.com/en-wl/wordlist/v1/other/enable2k.zip
         (the ENABLE2K archive as kept in the en-wl/wordlist repository, branch v1)
SHA-256: 44e2dd5c16c14336e8d4075a2f625af55a47cbb8b9f9ebcb7fcc3b5b1e1f0e5e  (enable2k.zip, 483122 bytes)
         f1e9b50e535a31d0d8451f7b373bb7966637d225e256a7c485983a0c7545fcd3  (WORD.LST inside it)
Steps:   1. Unzip, take WORD.LST (one lower-case word per line, CRLF).
         2. Keep the lines that match ^[a-z]{5}$ (8,672 words) and upper-case them.
         3. Add every word of en-answers.txt (all of them are in ENABLE), sort, remove duplicates (8,672 words).

en-answers.txt: SCOWL / English Speller Database, size 35
=====================================================================================================================
Source:  https://github.com/en-wl/wordlist, branch v2, commit 1e5b7d3a72f47a71da5d28686c1dd4b397178485 (2026-06-24)
         downloaded as https://codeload.github.com/en-wl/wordlist/tar.gz/1e5b7d3a72f47a71da5d28686c1dd4b397178485
SHA-256: aaecc7a54af576057e30b22a19b78bad011f2181f7e368ff3b736fbecbdfd4d0  (the tar.gz, 2314429 bytes)
         a83ce3874e4ac7636e7e27299241e819cf1207bdb8efbebf7940fd9f57533ae3  (the word list of step 2 below)
Steps:   1. Unpack, run `make` (creates scowl.db; Python 3 and its sqlite3 module).
         2. ./scowl word-list 35 A 1 --deaccent --wo-poses=abbr  (American spelling, variant level 1, no
            abbreviations, accents removed; 43,731 lines).
         3. Keep ^[a-z]{5}$ (3,452 words).
         4. Drop plain plurals and third-person forms: a word ending in S, ES or IES whose stem (minus S, minus ES,
            IES -> Y) is in the same list (2,303 words left).
         5. Hand-trimmed after reading the whole list: Roman numerals, abbreviations, informal contractions
            (gonna, wanna), crude, sexual, offensive and slur words, a few proper names (roman, china, maria),
            irregular plurals (women, geese, wives, radii), obscure comparatives and past forms (apter, baled,
            hided), and very obscure words (frond, udder, fetid), and the words of a second review: slave, prick,
            moron, thong, plain comparatives (icier, saner, paler), obscure inflections (foxed, hewed, burro).
            2,016 words were left.
         6. Base forms only, like the German list: removed regular past forms and -ING forms whose stem is an English
            word (ached, acted, added, aging, icing, owing, dying, lying and so on; the stem is looked up in ENABLE and
            the size-35 list). Kept: irregular past forms that are words of their own (began, broke, wrote) and words
            used mainly as adjectives or nouns (bored, tired, naked, mixed, fixed, jaded, armed, famed, vexed, muted,
            speed, breed, greed, creed, thing, fling, shred, tweed). 1,868 words are left.

de-answers.txt: Leipzig Corpora Collection, German news 2023, 1 million sentences
=====================================================================================================================
Source:  https://downloads.wortschatz-leipzig.de/corpora/deu_news_2023_1M.tar.gz (built 2024-01-11), file
         deu_news_2023_1M/deu_news_2023_1M-words.txt (id TAB word TAB frequency)
SHA-256: 392065f34be70e3612d6ef6b3edc6687b026524e57e00eef47f0528456deb1ba  (the tar.gz, 229132821 bytes)
Steps:   1. Apply the ß -> SS replacement and upper-casing of de-allowed.txt to every corpus word; add the
            frequencies of words that become equal.
         2. Keep the words that are in de-allowed.txt (5,024) and order them by frequency; take the first 4,500.
         3. Hand-picked from that list (rest dropped): nouns in the nominative singular, verbs in the infinitive,
            adjectives and adverbs uninflected, and a few function words and loanwords established in German
            (COVER, SUITE, HOBBY, COACH, SNACK and so on). Dropped: inflected forms, plurals, names, abbreviations,
            English words not established in German, variant spellings, offensive and crude words, drug slang and
            obscure, technical or regional words. The news corpus yields few base forms of five letters, so the
            list is smaller than the English one; 1,348 words are left.
Use:     Only the word frequencies of this corpus were used, to rank candidate words. de-answers.txt contains
         no corpus text and no frequency data, only words that are also in de-allowed.txt (CC0).
Terms:   The Leipzig Corpora Collection asks users to cite
